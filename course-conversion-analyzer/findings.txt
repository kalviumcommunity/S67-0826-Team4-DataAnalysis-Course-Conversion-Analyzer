Finding 1:
The high-view, low-conversion courses have a much lower average conversion rate than the other courses in the dataset (about 0.0164 vs 0.0722).

Finding 2:
The high-view, low-conversion courses also have a higher average price than the other courses (about 2749 vs 1682.33).

Finding 3:
The problematic courses have a lower average rating than the rest of the dataset (about 3.80 vs 4.1833).

Finding 4:
Preview-to-enrollment conversion is substantially lower for the problematic courses than for the other courses (about 0.0534 vs 0.1543).

Note:
These are associations observed in a very small dataset, not proof of causation.
